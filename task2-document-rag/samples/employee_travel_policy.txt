# CORPORATE EMPLOYEE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY (REV 2025.1)
Document ID: POL-TRAV-2025 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Approved by: Chief Financial Officer & People Operations Committee

## Section 1: Scope and Applicability
This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contracted personnel traveling on behalf of Apex Global Operations. All business travel must be pre-approved by the department manager at least 14 business days prior to departure.

## Section 2: Air Transportation
All domestic flights under 5 hours must be booked in Standard Economy Class. Premium Economy or Business Class is permitted exclusively for international flights with a continuous flight duration exceeding 8 hours, subject to written approval from the Vice President. Upgrades purchased at employee discretion are non-reimbursable.

## Section 3: Lodging and Hotel Limits
Standard maximum lodging allowances per night (excluding taxes and statutory fees):
- Tier 1 Metro Cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): Up to $350.00 per night.
- Tier 2 Cities (Chicago, Seattle, Austin, Berlin): Up to $240.00 per night.
- All Other Locations: Up to $180.00 per night.
Employees are required to reserve accommodations through the corporate booking portal. Incidental expenses such as hotel pay-per-view movies, spa treatments, and mini-bar consumption will not be reimbursed.

## Section 4: Meals and Per Diem
Apex reimburses actual meal expenses up to a maximum daily aggregate limit of $85.00 for domestic travel and $115.00 for international travel. The recommended allocation is:
- Breakfast: $20.00
- Lunch: $25.00
- Dinner: $40.00
Itemized receipts must be retained for every individual meal transaction exceeding $25.00. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized business entertainment dinner with clients.

## Section 5: Ground Transportation and Rideshare
Employees are encouraged to utilize public transit or standard rideshare services (UberX, Lyft Standard). Luxury rides (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) and rental cars require prior authorization. Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use is set at $0.67 per mile.

## Section 6: Expense Submission and Reimbursement Timeline
All travel expense reports must be submitted via the Concur portal within 30 days of the travel end date. Late submissions exceeding 60 days are subject to automatic forfeiture unless exceptional medical circumstances are documented.
