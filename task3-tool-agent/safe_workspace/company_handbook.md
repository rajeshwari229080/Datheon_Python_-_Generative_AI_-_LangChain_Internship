# Apex AI Engineering Handbook & Operational Standards

## 1. Remote Work Stipend
Every full-time team member receives an annual technology stipend of $2,500.00 for workspace ergonomic equipment, monitors, and compute hardware.

## 2. On-Call Rotation Compensation
Engineers participating in the primary P1/P2 production on-call rotation receive an incident availability stipend of $650.00 per 7-day shift, plus standard overtime rates for off-hours triage.

## 3. Continuous Learning & Conference Budget
Each employee is allotted $3,000.00 annually toward attending academic or industrial conferences (e.g. NeurIPS, ICML, KubeCon) and certified training programs.
